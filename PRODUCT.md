## Platform

web

## Users

Business decision-makers in Indonesia seeking digital product development for their businesses.

## Product Purpose

Wreative designs and develops websites, web applications, and mobile applications for businesses. Success means helping a prospective client start a qualified project inquiry through a free WhatsApp consultation.

## Positioning

The website presents Wreative as a development agency that delivers responsive, performant digital products with modern UI, clean and maintainable code, SEO-friendly websites, scalable systems, and post-launch maintenance. A distinct mechanism or differentiator beyond these published claims is undecided.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients review service information, portfolio links, testimonials, and FAQs, then contact Wreative via WhatsApp. The published delivery workflow covers requirements discussion, planning, UI/UX design, development, testing, and launch. Maintenance is available after release.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Website development includes company profiles, landing pages, custom websites, and internal systems.
- Web application development includes dashboards, SaaS products, ERP/CRM, and management systems.
- Mobile application development covers Android and iOS, including cross-platform development with Flutter.
- The website describes free consultation and project-specific proposals, scope, pricing, and timelines.
- Current public channels include WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Wreative.
- The public website is Indonesian-language.
- Legal entity named on the website: PT. Wreative Digital Solutions Indonesia.

## Product Principles

- Understand the client's business needs and goals before choosing a solution.
- Establish scope, plan, and timeline before development begins.
- Deliver digital products that are responsive and maintainable.
- Support clients beyond launch through ongoing maintenance.
